# A Day At The Spa

> Deep in the Wetlands of Morjord, the Dahl Athenaeum, sits in the massive grounds of the old Dahl family mansion. Our heroes find themselves searching for a valuable book to quell a great noble's desires. The soft candles keeps their curiousity as they find a mystery is afoot. Soon our heroes will enjoy, a day at the spa.

This campaign will be a 4-5 session adventure taking place entirely at level 5. The following are requirements specific to this campaign to keep in mind when building your character

## Campaign Specific Requirements

### Your Character's Personality
Along with _Personality Traits_, _Ideals_, _Bonds_ and _Flaws_, You will be required to provide the DM with a flaw your character thinks of themselves (1). This could be age (wishing to be young again, or maybe the opposite, wishing everyone around you regarded you as a mature adult), physical appearance (wishing you were taller, more muscular, missing that scar from your childhood), or your abilities (quicker wit, wiser or more coordinated, something tied directly to one of the core D&D stats).

### Team Interaction
At the start of the campaign the party will already have been together for some time. However, with some members you will have a history longer than your group. You will need at least 3 prior positive relationships with the other party members and will have to collaborate with them to do so (2). This could be siblings (or even parents), old childhood friends, ex-mercenaries who went out on your own etc. You cannot be enemies with your fellow party members but in some cases the rival dynamic could be appropriate. E.g. if you roleplay your rivalry as trying to out do each other in usefulness (think, volunteering to do a task first to show up the other) this is beneficial to the party but allows you to have that rivalry. If the roleplay is about messing with the other party member and intentionally hurting them in game, this is not an appropriate way to roleplay and is forbidden.

Since you all are already part of a party, you need to choose a name (3) that the commoners refer you by. You must also choose the event/adventure/task that gave you this name (4) and this will serve as the basis for reaching level 5. This team name and event is a group effort

*In total, there are 4 requirements specific to this campaign you must complete as part of character creation*

## Shopping Budget

Later on in the character creation section, you will be given an opportunity to shop for items to fill out your inventory. For this campaign your individual budget will be 625gp. It is recommended to leave some left over for during the campaign. You _are_ allowed to donate/pool your money together to buy a more powerful item.

## Campaign Beginnings

You have been tasked by Lord Bergius with finding a mirrored book written by Sylvarie Silversong. You arrive at the Dahl Athenaeum and are greeted by Lorris Niss.
