# Feats
Feats are an optional rule in D&D. They allow you to forgo your Ability Score Improvement, when available, to instead take a feat. You can choose to take any feat from the approved book list. For anything outside, talk to me, your DM!
 
Another optional rule we will use is choosing a feat at Level 1! Lets you add a little extra punch to your low-level characters. Some feats however will be banned for level 1, they are very powerful for this early in the game but you still have the opportunity to grab it at Level 4. Please remember that the maximum stat you can have at level 1 is 18. If a feat increases a stat above 18 then you do not gain that bonus.
